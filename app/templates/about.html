About Rashmi Packagers

A legacy of quality packaging from the heart of Kolkata

Who We Are

Rashmi Packagers is a Kolkata-based PP woven sack manufacturer with years of experience in producing high-quality packaging solutions for industries across West Bengal and beyond.

We specialize in manufacturing PP bags for cement, foam, and other industrial materials — handling everything from raw PP roll cutting and resizing to final stitching and dispatch.

With a team of 15+ skilled labourers and a fully operational factory at 5A, Lala Babu Lane, Kolkata, we are equipped to handle both small sample orders and large bulk requirements.
